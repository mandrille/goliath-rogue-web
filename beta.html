Goliath Rogue on Android

Closed beta — looking for a handful of testers

Goliath Rogue is coming to Android, and it needs a small group of people willing to play it on a phone for a couple of weeks before it can go live.

Why I'm asking. Google requires a new developer account to run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 continuous days before the game is even allowed to apply for a public listing. That's the rule standing between this game and the Play Store. Twelve people is the whole hurdle.

What you'd be signing up for

  • Install it and keep it installed for two weeks. The 14 days have to be continuous — if you uninstall, that tester slot resets.
  • Actually play it now and then. Not every day. But testers who install and never open the game are the single most common reason these applications get rejected, so genuine playing is the thing that matters.
  • Tell me anything you notice. Bugs, confusing bits, something that felt bad on a touchscreen. One sentence is fine. Brutal is fine.

It's free, there's no account or sign-up in the game, and it stays free afterwards.

How to join

  1. Email me the Gmail address on your Android phone. goliathstudiossp@gmail.com
    It has to be the Google account your phone actually uses for the Play Store — Google matches the tester list against that exact address, and any other one silently won't work.
  2. Wait for my reply. I add you to the tester list by hand. The link below does nothing until I have.
  3. Then open the opt-in link and accept.
    Become a tester
    If this shows an error page, it means step 2 hasn't happened yet — that's expected, not a broken link.
  4. Install from Play and go kill things.

Want to try it first?

Entirely reasonable. It plays in a browser, free, no download and no account — goliathe.itch.io/goliath-rogue. The Android build is the same game with touch controls.

The boring bits

  • The Android version is free and shows occasional ads between runs. The browser and itch.io versions don't.
  • What the game sends and doesn't send is written out in the privacy policy. Short version: nothing leaves your phone unless you put a name on the leaderboard.
  • You can leave the test at any time from the same opt-in link. No hard feelings — just tell me, so I know to find someone else.
